The problem

Visibility is worst when search matters most.

After floods and landslides, survivors are often stranded during storms, in fog, or after dark. Standard drone cameras struggle in exactly these conditions, and ground teams cannot cover large, cut-off areas quickly.

Rain, fog and haze

Weather washes out contrast in normal camera images, so people blend into the background.

Darkness

Many rescues happen at night, when ordinary RGB cameras see almost nothing.

Tiny targets

From the air, a person is only a few pixels wide, which is hard for both people and standard detectors to spot.

How it works

From the sky to the rescuer's phone in four steps.

SkySight's AI pipeline is designed to run on low-cost hardware, so it can be used by local response teams, not just well-funded agencies.

See

The drone captures the scene with three sensors: a regular camera, night vision and thermal.

Clear

Weather-adaptive enhancement cleans up rain, fog and low-light images, and the sensor views are fused into one clearer picture.

Detect

MERLIN-YOLO, our lightweight detector, is tuned to find small human figures from altitude.

Locate

Each person is tracked and geo-located, and their position is sent to the SkySight app for rescue teams.

The app

Every detection becomes a location rescuers can act on.

The SkySight Android app is the rescuer's side of the system. It turns what the drone sees into places on a map.

Survivor locations on a map

See where people were detected so teams know where to go first.

Coordinates you can share

Pass exact positions to boats, ground teams or command centres.
